## Implemented adaptations

- Preserve the full-width, warm Today view and the on-demand menu.
- Give current work a lavender surface, later activity thin borders and restrained semantic tints, and the checklist a contained panel. Keep arrows, labels, and icons so color is never the only cue.
- Add Tempo's own existing character to a useful setup/help card, not a competing floating mascot. Narrow layouts explicitly stack text and action.
- Add an optional three-choice questionnaire: name, coaching tone, and streak visibility. No diagnosis question. Texting consent, quiet hours, and timezone remain separate. Existing populated accounts get an invitation, while an empty account gets skippable setup. Settings reopens it.
- Show completion-based streak, today's completion count, and a seven-day expansion. A day counts after a task or routine step is actually completed; opening the app does not count. Yesterday's streak stays available throughout today. Hide it through setup/preferences. No fabricated rewards or negative messages after a break.
- Record routine completion days durably. Only known earlier completion dates can be backfilled; overwritten history is not invented.
- Offer task Undo and repair daily routine Undo. Keep reduced-motion support; no perpetual animation.

## Deliberately unchanged

Nutrition, meal planning, fitness, goals, and Calendar retain their established layouts. Today remains anchored to the current day; calendar navigation already offers a date rail. We did not add a second calendar just for decoration, mood tracking, copied Tiimo assets, or an unearned clinical claim.
