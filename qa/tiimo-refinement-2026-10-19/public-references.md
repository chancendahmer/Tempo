# Public UX references for Tempo

Reviewed official public documentation on 2026-10-19. Scope: Structured, Finch, and Todoist. These are documented product patterns, not observed signed-in sessions or evidence of clinical effectiveness. Recommendations below are design inferences for Tempo's warm cream/orange Today screen and on-demand menu. No accounts, purchases, paid API calls, or live-data changes were made.

## Five actionable ideas

1. **Make Today a place, with a compact date rail and one clear next action.** Structured opens on its daily timeline, with dates across the top; unscheduled tasks live separately in an inbox. For Tempo, use a seven-day rail with a clearly labeled selected day, then “What's next?” as the visual anchor. Morning/Afternoon/Evening pills can filter the day's existing plan, with counts and an empty-state explanation. Keep Later available through the menu. Treat the pills as Tempo's adaptation, not a verified Structured feature. Use orange only for selection/current action and quieter cream surfaces elsewhere. [Structured getting started](https://help.structured.app/en/articles/380546)

2. **Offer an editable starter routine instead of a long setup checklist.** Structured starts with wake/wind-down anchors and supports subtasks; Finch starts newcomers with a few goals and introduces additional features through quests. For Tempo, a dismissible “Make mornings easier” setup card can preview three steps and a time estimate, with “Make it mine” and “Maybe later.” Only create real routines after the user chooses them. A routine card can show “1 of 3 steps” plus an accessible progress bar and expandable next step. Do not prefill completed progress. [Structured getting started](https://help.structured.app/en/articles/380546), [Finch new-user guide](https://help.finchcare.com/hc/en-us/articles/42149821015693-New-User-Guide)

3. **Put a humane recovery action beside unfinished work.** Structured Replan offers reschedule, inbox, delete, or complete; Finch goals support pause, snooze, and edit. Tempo can expose “Choose a smaller step,” “Move to later,” and “Skip today” from each task's overflow menu. A small “Plans changed?” card can open a review of one item at a time. Keep rescheduling deliberate and confirm consequential changes; existing scheduling, consent, quiet-hours, and caps still apply. Avoid turning old tasks into a wall of red. [Structured Replan](https://help.structured.app/en/articles/4511874), [Finch goals](https://help.finchcare.com/hc/en-us/articles/37779940291213-Creating-and-Completing-Goals)

4. **Show small evidence of progress on Today; move the full history into the menu.** Todoist has a small daily-progress indicator and a separate daily/weekly Productivity view with completed-task history, editable goals, and break settings. Tempo can show “2 small steps today” with a subdued orange ring, then link to a weekly recap through “Your progress.” Make targets and celebration optional; a seven-day participation row is a gentler initial choice than an unbroken streak. Completed counts must come from real events and clearly state the period. [Todoist Productivity](https://www.todoist.com/help/todoist/features/use-the-productivity-view-in-todoist-6S63uAa9)

5. **Use a companion as quiet feedback, with user control.** Finch pairs small actions with a growing bird and adventure stories, while its Home keeps daily goals prominent and places other activities behind navigation. Tempo can use a small original companion illustration beside a welcome or completion message, with a brief warm response to a completed step. Keep expressive animation optional and respect reduced motion; avoid a pet that appears disappointed when users pause. Companion mood should be presentation, not an inferred mental-health assessment. [Finch overview](https://finchcare.com/about-finch), [Finch Home](https://help.finchcare.com/hc/en-us/articles/37780000231309-Exploring-the-Finch-Home-Page)

## Evidence and implementation limits

- Official docs establish that these patterns exist; they do not establish that the patterns reduce cognitive load or improve executive function for Tempo users. Test comprehension, perceived pressure, and ease of choosing the next action with actual users.
- Source layouts and availability differ by platform. Structured's Replan docs say it requires Pro and is not yet on Android or Web; this is inspiration for an interaction, not an integration promise.
- Finch's published streak design rewards checking in and includes repairs. Todoist allows celebrations to be disabled, breaks, vacation mode, and zero goals; its Karma also includes overdue penalties. Tempo should evaluate the control mechanisms separately from adopting the scoring system. [Finch new-user guide](https://help.finchcare.com/hc/en-us/articles/42149821015693-New-User-Guide), [Todoist Karma settings](https://www.todoist.com/help/todoist/features/introduction-to-karma-OgWkWy)
- Recommended order: Today hierarchy/date rail, editable routine card, recovery actions, factual weekly progress, companion polish. Do not ship fabricated streaks, decorative “live” progress, or buttons without meaningful destinations.
