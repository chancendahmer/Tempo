Read-only source audit of `d7e1a0e` on `codex/tempo-adaptive-home`. No live provider calls, production access, populated environment files, production migrations, service provisioning or production load. Historical QA results below were read, not rerun in this audit. Fresh targeted check: `npm run test -- src/server/domain/queue-health.test.ts src/server/db/repositories/operational-repository.integration.test.ts src/server/config/proactive-delivery.test.ts` passed three files / nine tests; repository integration fixtures use isolated PGlite.

## Decision

The current code has useful safety and durability foundations, but does not yet enforce a private 10-person admission boundary or provide evidence for 100-person capacity. A controlled ten-person pilot needs admission controls, a monitored operator/recovery path, verified provider capacity and a backup/restore drill. Expansion to 100 additionally needs a measured workload/latency envelope, cost controls and operating evidence. Existing synthetic tests must not be presented as carrier delivery or live AI evidence.

## Required external/operational evidence

- Ten people: founder/on-call owner; invite list and per-country availability; provider line/contact capacity, current commercial/compliance approval; dedicated real SMS signup/reply/reminder/STOP/START canary and callback evidence; actual worker safety settings; external readiness alert reaching the owner; journaled migrations and rollback; isolated restore drill plus encryption-key recovery and retention handling.
- One hundred: written provider capacity at 100 and rate/line limits; billing/spend alerts or caps; measured 100-account workload plus queue drainage and failure recovery; a staged cohort review with 48 hours of reliability/feedback evidence between expansions. Google Testing/account access limits must be checked operationally. These are not proven by local fixtures.
- Backups: `docs/OPERATIONS.md` requires Railway PITR and an isolated restoration drill. No restored-backup evidence was supplied by this audit. Code cannot prove hosted backup enablement or successful key recovery. Do not run a restore against production.

## Proposed ownership package

Own a **bounded isolated 100-account queue/overload drill** and its operator-facing evidence. Reuse real PostgreSQL, pg-boss, repositories, captured SMS transport and scripted parser; add no infrastructure or dependencies.

Suggested files: `scripts/pilot-load.ts`, `scripts/lib/pilot-load.ts` only if needed to keep the entrypoint cohesive, focused `src/server/testing/pilot-load.test.ts` for input/target guards, and `qa/pilot-2026-10-22/reliability-results.md`. Reuse the existing concurrency test rather than widening its scope. A separate small ops report may follow if another owner addresses admission/cost controls first.

Acceptance: target explicit localhost disposable `tempo_*` admin URL; create/drop only an exact run-owned random database; never fall back to DATABASE_URL or load env files; no real SDK/provider calls; sanitized aggregate JSON; maximum 100 accounts and bounded turns/runtime/concurrency; concurrent dispatchers, duplicate ingress, account chronology/isolation and exactly-one accepted outbound operation; controlled delayed/transient-failure cohort proving bounded active work and backlog drain while healthy accounts progress; assert zero stranded scheduled actions/messages/blocked keys after expected recovery. Record ingress/queue/completion latency p50/p95/max, elapsed time, throughput, retries, queue high-water mark, active concurrency high-water mark, final row-count invariants and limits. This demonstrates an isolated correctness/workload envelope only, not hosted or provider capacity.
