# Daily rhythm, Brain dump and setup on phones

The November 17, 2026 review used an isolated browser tab on the local fictional Alex preview. I tested at 390×844 and 320×568 CSS pixels. My customer scenarios were finding the evening routine after a shift, capturing an idea before it slipped away, and locating text preferences. The accessibility pass checked visible targets, native controls, focus after navigation, control labels and the shortest screen.

| Journey | Observed before editing | Change and observed retest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Routines | The morning checklist filled over a screen before Evening. The first timer was partly hidden behind the bottom navigation. [Before](01-routines-before.jpg) | A period switch brings either checklist into view. I created an evening routine, verified the suggested 21:00, completed the exact step, and saw 1 of 1. Saving with a different period selected that period's list. A follow-up check verified an untouched suggestion changed to 08:00 when choosing Morning and an explicitly entered 06:45 survived switching back to Evening. [Evening](11-evening-routine.jpg) |
| Sunrise and sunset | The primary action was near the bottom, and the phone layout removed the session art. [Before](02-light-before.jpg) | The mode switch and small sun or moon share a compact panel. I started Sunrise with a five-minute timer and Sunset with a 20-second preview, then ended both with the dialog control. The device and scheduled-alarm limitations remain visible. [Sunrise](12-sunrise-after.jpg) · [Sunset session](03-sunset-session.jpg) |
| Thoughts | Three introductions and a duplicate capture action pushed note content below the fold. [Before](04-thoughts-before.jpg) | Capture opens from the top bar. I saved a new thought and found it by a word in its body. The first two notes and their actions fit at 390px. [After](13-thoughts-after.jpg) |
| Memories and Ask | Explanatory headings repeated before the first fact. [Before](05-memories-before.jpg) | Filtered three memories to one, opened its correction draft and saw the original wording carried over. I edited the draft and pressed Send. Another reviewer who did not author these changes exercised them independently. Their simulated phone scores were Routines 9.2, Sunrise and sunset 9.1, Thoughts 9.2, Memories 9.0, Ask 9.0, Settings 9.1 and onboarding 9.2. They found an evening editor with a morning placeholder during their pass. I corrected it and verified the displayed placeholder in a fresh browser session. See their independent report for their scope.

Six focused test files passed (41 tests), along with source lint and typecheck during implementation. The evidence covers fictional interactions in Chromium. Authenticated storage, paid chat, outbound SMS, sound on a physical handset, background alarms and iPhone assistive technology were outside this test.
