After the fix I repeated the recipe-to-meal journey and verified Fitness and Nutrition both opened November 17 while returning to Meal plan restored November 28. I also rerated Fitness after the introductory empty block was removed and the signup form after the consent-order and social-proof changes.

This pass used browser preview state. Live account persistence, phone verification, SMS, food-provider lookup, device keyboard and camera were outside this test.
