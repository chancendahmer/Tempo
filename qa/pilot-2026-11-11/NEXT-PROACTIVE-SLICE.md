## Later targets need an explicit action and time

| Workspace area | Eligible user request |
| --- | --- |
| Meal plan | Choose a saved meal and a check-in window for prep or shopping. The plan's date and meal slot alone do not authorize a contact or establish that food was eaten. |
| Workout plan | Choose one saved session and a day and time window for getting started. A saved plan and a dated activity log describe different things. The check-in neither records a workout nor assumes it happened. |
| Thought | Choose a specific Thought, a revisit window and an optional SMS-safe label. Default to a generic link. Keep the note body in the workspace. |

Routine permission does not extend to these targets, food logs, nutrition targets, hardware or health signals. A request for a text at an exact time continues to use the existing reminder command. ## Acceptance before a hosted canary

- A fictional account with zero tasks opts in to one saved routine and produces one eligible occurrence in shadow, then one captured send in an isolated worker test. Both the routine and its first unchecked step keep their saved state after the text and workspace navigation.
- A matching fixture with routine consent off produces no review or send. Test each suppression independently, including global opt-out, STOP, quiet hours, Calendar busy or stale, active focus, a competing task or cleanup contact, and a cap already reached. Repeat late opt-out, routine edit and step checkoff immediately before submission.
- Replay evaluation, worker retry and a restart after outbound reservation. Test a midnight timezone boundary and both daylight-saving transitions. Require one review reservation and at most one provider submission per eligible local occurrence. A confirmed rejection ends the occurrence. Keep unknown attempts pending for reconciliation and unsent drafts out of customer history.
- Mock budget denial and malformed review. Both must produce zero sends. Check the outgoing review payload and SMS for raw notes, routine titles, food or exercise logs and other unrelated personal content. Validate with the normal test, lint, typecheck and release checks before a separately authorized hosted demo-account test.
