## Customer path

1. **Find Tempo and attempt to join.** At the hosted checkpoint, a new uninvited person cannot enroll. The deployed baseline advertises early access and presents the phone and recurring-text consent form before the POST can reject them as uninvited. The rejection gives a way forward for someone with an organizer. A person who discovered the public site independently reaches a dead end. Pricing links advertised three choices, but each opened the same phone form and the form sent no plan data.
