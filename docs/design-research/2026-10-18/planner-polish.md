# Planner navigation and calm interaction research

Research date: 2026-10-18. Scope: publicly accessible official Tiimo, Todoist, Motion, Sunsama, and Structured sources, interpreted for Tempo's existing neutral sidebar with four navigation groups and section color identity. Audience: people who benefit from external structure and predictable interfaces. These are design recommendations, not clinical outcome claims.

## Evidence and limits

Official product screenshots were visually inspected in an agent-owned background browser tab. Help pages and changelogs were also read. No accounts were created, no authenticated product sessions were entered, and no live task/calendar editing was tested. This is competitor research, not an end-to-end product audit. Marketing images can lag shipped UI: the Tiimo laptop photo visibly uses February 2024, and Motion's official embedded navigation reference shows August–September 2025. They establish composition, not guaranteed current pixel specifications.

## Motion: documented versus observed

- **Documented app behavior:** Todoist's [2026 changelog](https://www.todoist.com/help/todoist/product-updates/2026-changelog-HD3jJAtLd) describes animated folder icons when clicked, an Android Display menu animation repaired so options appear with the sheet, and Android Ramble honoring disabled motion effects. It also records layout-stability fixes, including reserving space for illustrations and preventing content shifts when closing a narrow sidebar. These are vendor statements, not behavior reproduced in this run.
- **Actually observed:** Structured's public marketing section was faint immediately after scrolling and fully visible in the next screenshot, consistent with a scroll-reveal treatment. This observation concerns the marketing page only. The Motion Supademo reference loaded a staged screenshot and callout; that is not native interaction evidence.
- **Not established:** No reliable core-app easing curves, durations, completion animations, navigation transitions, or reduced-motion handling were measured across these products. No competitor timing values should be attributed to this research.
- **External accessibility grounding:** [W3C's explanation of SC 2.3.3](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/animation-from-interactions) supports allowing people to disable nonessential interaction motion and honoring reduced-motion preferences. This is accessibility guidance, not evidence of ADHD treatment benefit or proof of any competitor's compliance.

## Five implementable recommendations

1. **Keep the four groups as a fixed map.** Preserve existing names and order, use understated group labels and consistent row/icon alignment, and let the content pane carry the page's main hierarchy. Use a filled selected-row background plus font weight and `aria-current="page"`. Do not depend on color alone. This adapts Todoist/Motion's clear destination grouping to Tempo without adding more navigation layers.
2. **Give section color a small, consistent job.** Keep the sidebar's surface and unselected text neutral. Carry each section's identity through its selected icon/accent and the page's small section marker, using the existing palette. Pair every color with text/icon meaning. Reserve warning/error colors for actual statuses. Structured's activity colors and Todoist's small project colors suggest recognition cues; a full multicolor rail would compete with task content.
3. **Make one primary next action obvious.** On each Tempo screen, lead with a readable heading, one short explanation, and its main action; subordinate supporting metadata, controls, and settings. Keep controls in familiar places and make optional detail reveal on request. This follows Sunsama's primary/secondary panel roles and Tiimo's capture-to-plan relationship. Avoid turning every empty state or tip into an equally prominent card.
4. **Use motion to confirm changes locally.** Proposed Tempo values, not competitor measurements: about 120–180 ms for hover/press color and opacity changes, about 160–220 ms for a small popover or drawer, no bounce or full-screen route slide. Animate only the element whose state changed; keep the sidebar and page title stationary. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, remove transforms, entrance movement, and looping decoration; preserve immediate visible state feedback. Avoid `transition: all` and permanent shimmer/pulsing.
5. **Protect orientation and control.** Preserve row dimensions and reserve space for async content; keep navigation selected state visible during loading. Confirm saves near the originating action with persistent text/status where needed, rather than shifting content or relying on motion. Keep keyboard focus apparent, retain meaningful action labels, and restore focus after dismissing overlays. Keep task counts restrained; Todoist's option to hide counts is evidence that numeric load need not dominate navigation. These are plausible executive-function supports that require user testing, not proven clinical effects.

## Review checks for the implementation

At desktop and mobile sizes, a user should be able to identify the current section without reading the whole rail; group order should stay stable; selecting a destination should not move unrelated controls; all labels should remain readable at 200% zoom; keyboard focus should remain visible; and reduced-motion mode should remove decorative movement. Confirm actual focus behavior, contrast, and motion in Tempo's running interface before claiming them as validated.
